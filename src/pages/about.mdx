## Communities

I co-organise [SciPy India](https://scipy.in), and I'm helping restart [Write the Docs India](https://write-the-docs-india.github.io) with Sujatha Mohan and Agriya Khetarpal. The three of us co-ran the Documentation and Technical Writing devroom at [IndiaFOSS 2026](/posts/indiafoss-2026-experience/). You'll also find me around PyCon India, FOSS United, BangPypers and ChennaiPy events, usually at a booth.

## Talks and Writing

My talks so far range from incremental data pipelines with CocoIndex at Rust Delhi to [technical writing in the age of AI](/posts/wikimedia-dsdp-experience/) at Wikimedia DSDP. The full list is on [/talks](/talks), and the [blog](/posts) has the rest: developer tools, notebooks, and the events I end up at.
